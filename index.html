Marks are converted to Grade Points on KMU's own scale: 90–100 = A+ (4.0), 80–89 = A (4.0), 75–79 = B+ (3.5), 70–74 = B (3.0), 64–69 = C+ (2.5), 60–63 = C (2.0), below 60 = F (0.0) — KMU's semester system requires at least 60% to pass a course.
